Vita Mango helps you build a daily supplement routine with Jelly, a cheerful mango slime.

■ Track today’s supplements
See everything you plan to take today at a glance and check each item off in seconds.

■ Your personal supplement list
Add the supplements you take, set individual times, and edit them whenever you need.

■ One-line wellness journal
Leave a short note about how you feel today. Jelly remembers your entries and reacts with a matching expression.

■ Personalized guidance, processed on your device
An adaptive questionnaire asks only the questions that apply to you, then suggests a reference combination designed to avoid overlapping benefits. Review your wellness chart and the reason behind each suggestion.

■ Grow Jelly and complete your collections
Finish your daily routine to earn experience and help Jelly grow. Build a streak and unlock new expressions and supplement entries.

No account or server is required. Your supplements, journal, survey results, and Jelly progress stay on your device. You can delete everything from My Jelly, and removing the app also removes its saved data.

※ Vita Mango provides general wellness information only and does not replace medical diagnosis or treatment. If you have a health condition, take medication, or are pregnant or breastfeeding, consult a qualified healthcare professional before adding a supplement or changing how you take one.
